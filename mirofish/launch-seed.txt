1. THE PRODUCT
======================================================================

What it is
Kansenradar is a Belgian B2B intelligence service for marketing and communication agencies in Flanders. It monitors public procurement notices (government tenders) and sends one curated e-mail digest every Monday at 07:00 with only the tenders that are relevant for a marketing/communication agency: campaigns, marketing, PR, events and websites. Each tender gets a short summary (what is asked, budget if known, deadline, region, link to the full file) and a relevance score out of 10.

It is deliberately not a platform: no login, no dashboard. "Wij monitoren, jij beslist." (We monitor, you decide.)

How it works (behind the scenes)
- Sources: TED (Tenders Electronic Daily, the EU publication platform) today. The Belgian e-Procurement / e-Notification feed is planned but not connected yet (API key still to be requested).
- Filter on CPV codes (EU procurement categories) for advertising, marketing, PR, events and website design.
- AI pre-screening and AI summaries, then a weekly selection.
- The landing page also promises a "human check", filtering on the agency's own sector and region, deadline and budget alerts, and "subsidies, coming soon". These four promises are not built yet and are still under review.

Who makes it
An initiative of Autom8ted, a small Belgian automation company, built from Antwerp. Contact via a general mailbox at kansenradar.be.

7. CONTEXT
======================================================================

- Market: Flanders, Belgium. The simulated customers are Dutch-speaking (Flemish) agency owners and employees, with Flemish names, living and working in Flemish municipalities. All prices are in euro. They read Flemish Dutch and expect "je/jij" tone, no hype.
